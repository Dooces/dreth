# Glossary

## nethra

A reusable handle or factoring that helps Dreth decide which distinctions are
worth considering in a specific scope. A nethra is not a final truth object;
it is a graph-mediated structure whose usefulness is earned through evidence.

## cert / certificate

The current implementation record of earned authority. A certificate records
scope, context, operation, role, and evidence counts. It is not proof of truth.

The class name `NethraCertificate` remains for compatibility, but the intended
meaning is "earned authority record", not final certification.

## authority

Operational permission to prefer, reuse, route through, skip, or propagate a
nethra in a scope. Authority is earned by evidence and can be narrowed or
defeated by later evidence.

## relative authority

Authority measured against local alternatives under a context and consequence
budget. A nethra can be authoritative in one context and weak or unusable in
another.

## best available nethra

The most trusted current filter or handle among the local competitor set. It may
still be fallible and may be replaced when a better alternative appears.

## dormant alternative

A failed, beaten, or currently unused nethra retained as contrast, search
guidance, or a future fallback. Dormant alternatives are not treated as active
authority, but they can remain informative.

## exception boundary

A localized limit on where a nethra applies. An exception boundary preserves
useful authority outside the failed scope instead of forcing universal
revocation.

## revocation

Removal or weakening of an earned authority record when evidence shows that its
authority should no longer be used in the relevant scope. Revocation is an
operational repair action, not a metaphysical verdict.

## trass

Operational equivalence within a scope. If substituting a distinction leaves the
monitored targets unchanged in that scope, Dreth may treat the distinction as
trass there. Trass is not universal sameness.

## tareth

A consequential distinction relative to interventions, predictions, or actions.
If substitution changes monitored targets in the tested scope, Dreth treats the
distinction as tareth there. Tareth is not an absolute ontological class.

## nethra-of-nethra

A higher-order reuse or filter structure that narrows which alternatives are
worth considering. It can guide search across nethras without making any lower
or higher level authority absolute.
