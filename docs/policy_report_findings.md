# Policy Report Findings

## 1. Scope

Report based on `reports/policy_report.tsv`, generated by running all three provider
policies across a factorial grid of schedules, variable counts, and cycle lengths.

| Parameter  | Values                                          |
|------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| schedules  | `false_trass`, `regime_switch`                  |
| n\_vars    | 50, 75, 100                                     |
| cycles     | 5 000, 7 500, 10 000                            |
| seeds      | 42, 99, 7 (3 runs per policy cell; 162 total runs, 54 policy-report rows) |
| policies   | `sensitivity/none`, `history/history`, `history_rescue/history_rescue` |

Each cell is the mean of 3 independent seeds. All comparisons are relative to
`sensitivity/none` as the baseline within each (schedule, n\_vars, cycles) group.

---

## 2. Invariant status

All 162 runs passed invariants.

Policy scoring and summarization are **diagnostic only**. The summarizer
(`scripts/summarize_policy_report.py`) reads the TSV offline and produces no side
effects on agent behaviour. No runtime policy switching is implemented in this report.

---

## 3. Findings

### 3.1 Schedule-level winners

| Schedule        | Recommended policy               | Win count (of 9 groups) |
|-----------------|----------------------------------|-------------------------|
| `false_trass`   | `history_rescue/history_rescue`  | 5 of 9                  |
| `regime_switch` | `sensitivity/none`               | 8 of 9                  |

Winner is determined by lowest mean `quality_cost` within each
(schedule, n\_vars, cycles) group.

### 3.2 history\_rescue behaviour

- Consistently reduces IV (`mean_delta_iv` negative across all false\_trass cells).
- Increases audit count, revocations, and unique failures relative to
  `sensitivity/none`, particularly as `n_vars` grows.
- In `regime_switch` worlds the audit and failure cost dominates, making
  `sensitivity/none` cheaper overall.

### 3.3 history/history behaviour

- Competitive with `history_rescue` at small `n_vars` / short `cycles` under
  `false_trass`, but does not hold the lead reliably.
- Under `regime_switch` it is the most expensive policy in 8 of 9 groups.
- Should be treated as experimental; it is not a candidate for a default.

### 3.4 Pareto / dominated rows

- `sensitivity/none` is marked dominated in 2 cells (both `false_trass`, low
  `n_vars`), confirming that the history-aware policies can dominate on multiple
  metrics simultaneously in narrow conditions.
- No `history/history` or `history_rescue` rows are ever dominated.

---

## 4. Interpretation

**Provider utility is schedule-dependent.** The right policy under `false_trass`
is not the right policy under `regime_switch`. A single global default cannot be
optimal across both regimes.

**Sensitivity remains the conservative default.** It wins decisively under
`regime_switch` and never incurs the extra audit / revocation overhead that
history-aware policies carry. This is operational guidance for the tested
scopes, not proof that a policy is universally correct.

**history\_rescue is useful for false\_trass-like worlds.** Its advantage comes
from lower intervention volume, at the cost of modestly more
audits and revocations. That trade-off is worth it when the world is stable enough
that trass events are informative rather than noisy.

**No evidence justifies automatic policy switching or learned residual gating.**
The schedule identity is not observable at runtime without oracle knowledge.
Switching on observed regime signals risks instability and has not been validated.
The findings support offline guidance only.

---

## 5. Reproduction

Generate the raw TSV (approx. 10–20 minutes depending on hardware):

```bash
python scripts/batch_run.py \
  --schedule false_trass,regime_switch \
  --vars 50,75,100 \
  --cycles 5000,7500,10000 \
  --seeds 42,99,7 \
  --policy-report \
  --policy-report-tsv reports/policy_report.tsv \
  --hybrid-control interfaces \
  --repair-agenda
```

Summarize offline (reads the TSV, no agent code touched):

```bash
python scripts/summarize_policy_report.py --tsv reports/policy_report.tsv
```
